Cindy is an **open-source AI agent, ready out of the box and yours to shape**.
Your computers, your phone, your models — connected through a companion that
can do real work in your files and apps.

### All your devices. One Cindy.

Continue a session from another device, access its files and use model providers
on your connected computers. Reach the full desktop from your phone. Keep your
computer online and enable remote access — no server rental or self-hosting needed.

### The agents you know. A companion of your own.

**Claude Code · Codex · Pi.** Three agent harnesses in one product, with model
choice, multi-agent teamwork and rich, interactive answers. Bring a supported
subscription, your API keys or local models; the client itself is free.

### Built in the open. Shaped by you.

Create companions with their own roles, memory, Skills and routines. Extend Cindy
with plugins, or help make the product better for everyone. A useful integration,
a thoughtful interaction, a bug report — it all counts.
